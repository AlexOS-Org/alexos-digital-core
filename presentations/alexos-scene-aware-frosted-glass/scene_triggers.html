03 · Scene triggers

Greeting scenes that respond to your context.

AlexOS moves beyond static backgrounds by using real-world signals to rotate the dashboard atmosphere.

Time of day

The default policy. Maps morning, day, evening, and night to specific visual moods.

Local weather

Uses Open-Meteo signals. Rain triggers Ocean waves; clear skies trigger Basketball energy.

Geolocation

Approximates mood based on latitude. Tropical zones favor Ocean; Northern zones favor Sunset.

Safe Fallback: If weather or location permissions are denied, the system automatically reverts to Time of Day rotation.
Morning / Rain Ocean Waves

Cool teal radial waves with deep blue saturation for a calm start.

Day / Clear Basketball Energy

Warm amber court-inspired circles for high-momentum operations.

Evening / Clouds Sunset Focus

Copper and rose horizon treatment for end-of-day review.

Night / Storm Night Focus

Low-light navy field with subtle violet pools for focused planning.
