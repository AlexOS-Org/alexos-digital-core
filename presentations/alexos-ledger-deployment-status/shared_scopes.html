02 / Money Center scopes
One expense. One scope. One explanation.
Common household costs can be shared without being misclassified as business profit.
Personal

Home & family

Purely personal outflows remain outside business profitability.

  • Home water
  • Children’s school fees
  • Children’s shopping
  • Personal food and airtime
Shared

Common costs

Use a shared scope when home and work both benefit from the same expense.

  • Food — Shared
  • Water — Shared
  • Electricity — Shared
  • Internet, WiFi, airtime, rent
Business

Operating costs

Business costs require the business destination and are included in its expense view.

  • Ads — Business
  • Rider / delivery
  • Packaging and supplier
  • Office and platform costs
Transfers between accounts remain neutral: they change where cash sits, not profit or total net worth.
Source: AlexOS finance scope implementation · existing ledger fields
