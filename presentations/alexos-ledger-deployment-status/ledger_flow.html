01 / Ledger architecture
Money enters once.
Every movement stays explainable.
The same accounting invariant is used across DailyGear, personal finances, and business accounts.
Operating profit = recognised revenue − COGS − order costs − business expenses
01 · RECEIPT

Customer pays

One income transaction is posted to the selected receiving account after payment is confirmed.

02 · COSTS

Costs attach to the order

Supplier, delivery, packaging, advertising, and other order costs reduce contribution profit once recorded.

03 · TRANSFER

Money changes location

Bank to M-Pesa, business to personal, or reserve transfers move balances without becoming income or expenses.

04 · RESULT

Profit is calculated

Net daily profit is calculated from posted evidence; it is not posted a second time as cash.

Source: AlexOS Business Operations accounting invariants
