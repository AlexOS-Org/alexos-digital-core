# AlexOS SEO & Acquisition Plan

## Goal

Build organic discovery for AlexOS while keeping the authenticated product private. SEO exists to create qualified signups and eventually paid customers, not vanity traffic.

## Public vs private

### Public and indexable

The future marketing site should contain useful pages for real search intent, for example:

- AI business management software
- CRM for small businesses
- sales follow-up software
- AI sales assistant
- business finance management
- small business command center
- vehicle sales management
- product/e-commerce sales workflows

These are planning themes, not permission to create thin keyword pages.

### Private and not indexable

Authenticated dashboards, CRM records, customer data, financial data, tasks, business intelligence, and other private application screens should not be exposed to search engines.

## Technical SEO checklist

- [ ] Unique page titles.
- [ ] Useful meta descriptions.
- [ ] Canonical URLs.
- [ ] `robots.txt` policy.
- [ ] XML sitemap for public routes.
- [ ] Open Graph and social metadata.
- [ ] Structured data where appropriate.
- [ ] Semantic headings.
- [ ] Internal linking between related public pages.
- [ ] Fast page load and optimized assets.
- [ ] Mobile-first responsive layout.
- [ ] Search Console verification.
- [ ] Analytics and signup conversion tracking.

## Content strategy

Create pages that answer genuine customer problems. Avoid mass-generated pages with minimal unique value.

The public story should communicate the differentiator:

> AlexOS helps entrepreneurs decide what matters, follow up with customers, understand their money, and focus their effort on actions that can grow revenue.

## Acquisition funnel

```text
Search / social / referral
        ↓
Useful public page
        ↓
AlexOS value proposition
        ↓
Signup
        ↓
Activation
        ↓
First lead / first business workflow
        ↓
Meaningful outcome
        ↓
Retention
        ↓
Paid conversion
```

## Measurement

Track the complete funnel where technically and legally appropriate:

- organic visitors;
- landing-page engagement;
- signups;
- activation;
- leads created;
- follow-ups completed;
- sales recorded;
- revenue/profit outcomes;
- paid conversion.

SEO work should be prioritized by its expected contribution to qualified acquisition and revenue.
