# AlexOS Orion Roadmap

## Current milestone: Phase 3 — Revenue Command Center

The goal is to evolve AlexOS from a dashboard with connected modules into a system that actively helps Alex decide and execute the work most likely to create or protect money.

### Phase 3 sequence

1. **Validate current build** — lint, production build, desktop review, mobile review, Supabase/runtime checks.
2. **Revenue Pipeline** — lead → follow-up → opportunity → sale → profit.
3. **Mission & Tasks** — prioritize revenue, money protection, personal priorities, and important deadlines.
4. **Gmail signals** — identify important messages and propose tasks/CRM updates using least-privilege access.
5. **Facebook signals** — capture relevant page inquiries and lead opportunities where supported.
6. **Market & Ad Intelligence** — combine internal performance with external market evidence to recommend what to sell and what to advertise.
7. **SEO & acquisition** — public marketing site, search intent pages, technical SEO, analytics and conversion tracking.
8. **Monetization** — prove AlexOS creates value for Alex first, then introduce paid tiers/services only when justified.

## After Phase 3

### Phase 4 — Closed-loop revenue intelligence

- Campaign → lead → sale → profit attribution.
- Product/vehicle opportunity scoring.
- Margin-aware promotion recommendations.
- Customer segment and repeat-purchase insights.
- Orion recommendation history and outcome tracking.

### Phase 5 — Multi-business operating system

- Clear business workspaces without fragmenting the core experience.
- Business-specific dashboards and pipelines.
- Shared customer and financial intelligence where appropriate.
- Business-level permissions and integrations.

### Phase 6 — Productization

Only after AlexOS works for Alex:

- Public onboarding.
- Subscription plans.
- Tenant/account isolation.
- Billing.
- Product analytics.
- Documentation and support.
- Scalable public web experience.

## Explicitly not next

- Building a second CRM beside the existing CRM.
- Adding random charts merely for visual density.
- Large paid automation stacks before revenue exists.
- Broad AI features without a measurable user outcome.
- Major database rewrites without an audit.
