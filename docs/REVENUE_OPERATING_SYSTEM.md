# AlexOS Revenue Operating System

## Objective

The revenue system is the core differentiator for AlexOS. It should help Alex decide what to work on, what to sell, who to target, what offer/ad to test, and what actually made money.

## Core loop

```text
Market evidence
      ↓
Product / vehicle / offer choice
      ↓
Target customer
      ↓
Ad / content / landing page
      ↓
Lead
      ↓
Qualification
      ↓
Follow-up
      ↓
Sale
      ↓
Revenue - costs = profit
      ↓
Outcome stored
      ↓
Orion learns from evidence
```

## Business-specific intelligence

### DailyGear
Track product availability, inquiries, price, estimated margin, ad source, conversion and repeat demand. Recommend tests rather than assuming every available product deserves advertising spend.

### BFSUMA
Track product interest, customer questions, orders, repeat demand, source and compliant offer angles. Product and health claims must be supported by appropriate documentation and should not be invented by Orion.

### Vehicle Sales
Track vehicle, dealer/source, estimated sale value, financing/deposit requirements, buyer demand, lead source, expected commission, conversion and deal outcome.

## Opportunity scoring

A first internal priority signal can use:

```text
Opportunity value × probability = weighted opportunity value
```

This is a prioritization aid, not a prediction guarantee. Later versions should incorporate recency, follow-up due date, margin, acquisition cost, customer fit, and historical conversion.

## Ad intelligence

The target model is:

```text
Ad spend
  → impressions / reach
  → clicks / conversations
  → leads
  → qualified leads
  → sales
  → revenue
  → gross profit
  → return on ad spend / return on spend
```

A high lead count is not automatically a successful campaign. Orion should favor profitable outcomes when reliable cost and revenue data exist.

## Market intelligence

External market analysis should be evidence-backed. Potential sources include search trends, competitor offers, local pricing, customer conversations, ad results, seasonality, inventory availability and credible market research.

AlexOS must distinguish:

- **Observed:** directly measured in AlexOS data.
- **Sourced:** supported by an external source.
- **Inferred:** a reasoned interpretation.
- **Unknown:** not enough evidence.

## Personal Mission

Revenue intelligence feeds Today's Mission. The system should prioritize:

1. Immediate high-value opportunities.
2. Revenue-generating actions.
3. Money-protection actions.
4. Time-sensitive personal commitments.
5. Important but lower-impact work.

The aim is not to maximize completed tasks. It is to maximize meaningful outcomes.
