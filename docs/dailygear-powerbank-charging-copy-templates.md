# DailyGear Power Banks & Charging Accessories
## SEO and Facebook-Compliant Copy Templates

**Purpose:** Use these templates for the first DailyGear batch without inventing product specifications, prices, availability, reviews, discounts or performance claims. Replace every bracketed field with a fact confirmed by the current supplier record, packaging, invoice, product page or physical inspection.

## Publication rule

Do not change a product to `active` until the evidence ledger contains a traceable current source, `availability_confirmed` is true, and the product has at least **15 available units for each SKU, colour and size/capacity variant**. Historical Meta or Instagram records can support product identity and wording, but they do not prove current stock, current price, delivery timing or returns terms.

## Copy safety rules

Use “fast charging,” “high capacity,” “compatible with,” “durable,” “original,” “safe,” “premium,” “best,” “limited,” “new,” “water-resistant,” “warranty included” or similar claims only when the exact claim is supported by current evidence. If the source states a numeric wattage, capacity or cable length, preserve the number accurately. If a fact is not verified, omit it instead of using a general substitute.

Do not add fake reviews, ratings, customer results, sales numbers, scarcity, countdowns, invented discounts, unverified original prices, unsupported medical or safety claims, or language that implies a personal attribute or fear. Keep Facebook ad wording factual, product-focused and free of sensational promises.

## Safe CTA library

Choose one CTA that matches the page and actual buying flow: `Shop Now`, `Add to Cart`, `Buy Now`, `Choose Your Colour`, `Choose Your Variant`, `View Specifications` or `Check Availability`. Do not place multiple competing primary CTAs above the fold.

## Final product-entry checklist

| Check | Required before publication |
|---|---|
| Product identity | Exact name, brand/model and category are supported by current evidence. |
| Variant identity | Every colour, size, capacity or model option has its own SKU where required. |
| Price | Current KES selling price is verified; discounts and original prices are legitimate. |
| Stock | Every publishable SKU/colour variant has at least 15 available units. |
| Availability | Current availability is confirmed in the product record. |
| Specifications | Capacity, wattage, ports, dimensions, material and compatibility claims are sourced. |
| Images | Product images are real or rights-cleared and accurately represent the item. |
| Delivery | Delivery area, fee and timing are confirmed before publication. |
| Returns | Return window and conditions are confirmed before publication. |
| Payment | Only genuinely available payment methods are displayed. |
| Evidence ledger | Source URL or document, source ID, observed date, factual excerpt, confidence and reconciliation status are recorded. |
| Copy review | No fake reviews, unsupported claims, manufactured scarcity or invented guarantees appear. |
