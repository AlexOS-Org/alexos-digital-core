# DailyGear Competitor Evidence

## Sources reviewed

- [Jumia Kenya](https://www.jumia.co.ke/): public site states it serves Nairobi and across Kenya, offers categories including electronics, computing, fashion, sports, health and beauty, and supports online ordering, credit cards, cash on delivery, delivery estimates, and returns/refunds. These are site claims, not independent performance evidence.
- [Sophie Kenya luggage and bags](https://sophie.co.ke/product-category/luggage-bags/): visible listings include Bange laptop bags at KSh 3,699–3,919, a 3-in-1 fibre travel suitcase discounted from KSh 15,000 to KSh 8,499, and Bull Captain bags at KSh 3,399–3,999. Page shows stock/filter/category merchandising.
- [Furaha Finds school supplies](https://www.furahafinds.com/collections/school-supplies): visible listings include rolling trolley school bags at KSh 4,499 sale from KSh 5,500, kids water bottles at KSh 1,899, graphic backpacks at KSh 2,899, 3-in-1 trolley bags at KSh 4,799, kids backpacks around KSh 1,999–3,399, and multiple themed school bags from KSh 2,099–3,299.
- [Sweech power banks](https://sweech.co.ke/power-banks/): page was accessible but returned only cookie/search shell text in extraction; no prices were used.

## Benchmark interpretation

Jumia competes through breadth, COD, delivery visibility, promotions, and marketplace trust signals. Sophie emphasizes category depth, brands, price ranges, sale pricing, and stock cues. Furaha Finds emphasizes a focused school-supplies assortment, product-specific SEO titles, visible sale/regular pricing, and child-focused categories. DailyGear should benchmark these structural patterns but not copy claims or infer demand from page presence alone.

## Research boundary

Search and page extraction did not verify Facebook follower counts, ad-active duration, or competitor conversion rates. Those values must remain `Not verified` until public pages or Ads Library records are independently inspected. No competitor product was imported into DailyGear from these pages.
