## Explicit exclusions and unresolved evidence

The connected Instagram feed also contains legacy non-DailyGear health-product posts. Those posts are not imported into DailyGear because the current task is scoped to the DailyGear retail catalogue and the platform must not introduce an obsolete business identity. A Car-Bar Motion.ke campaign was also returned from a broadly named Meta ad account and is excluded from DailyGear evidence.

The Meta Ads Manager ad-list response exposed ad IDs, names, campaign IDs, ad-set IDs, statuses, and dates, but the retrieved ad objects did not expose creative copy, destination URLs, or media fields. Consequently, historical ad names can support candidate discovery, but they are not sufficient by themselves for product copy, price, variant, or image import. Pixel/event data, Commerce Manager catalogue records, Facebook page records, and historical ad creative details remain unavailable through the currently enabled connector operations and must not be fabricated.

## Import gate

A candidate may be imported only after duplicate checks across title, SKU, source ID, source URL, image, variant attributes, and price. Each imported SKU or colour variant must also pass the existing 15-unit minimum stock rule before publication. Historical promotional language may inform copy positioning, but unsupported materials, dimensions, warranties, certifications, health claims, delivery promises, and technical specifications must not be promoted to current product facts.
